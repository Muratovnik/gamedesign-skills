# Harbour of Echoes: original design studies

These small, original, synthetic games demonstrate constructing and altering
playable relationships. They can be played from the written rules with paper,
tokens and, for the loom, a voice or tapping. The recorded author replays are
logical and arithmetic walkthroughs, not player studies or measured game feel.

## Choose a study

| Study | Start here | What changes between versions |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Committed action inside a spatial encounter | [Quay crossing](quay-crossing.md) | Shortening a real route restores an available response; a narrow gap still blocks it |
| New free expressive play | [Sound-and-light loom](free-form-loom.md) | Independent pitch/density control replaces a saturated mapping; two equal-duration phrases differ in form |
| World, perspective and investigation | [The third bell gamebook](gamebook.md) | A closed shutter removes a witness channel; a reserve tank changes work, traces and dialogue |
| Learning, permanent help and an external objective | [Lens workshop](lens-workshop.md), plus the gamebook's practice drum | A new proportion comparison replaces an answer-highlighted badge; permanent readout remains |

[Replay record](replay-notes.md) gives specific paths and outcomes. Machine-readable
[case inputs](cases.json) and [calculation output](calculation-output.json) identify
the narrow symbolic models used in the author's arithmetic checks. No random
seed is needed because those models are deterministic. The teaching examples are
public and unsuitable as undisclosed final evaluation cases.

## Connect the study to the executable examples

- **Lantern Quay:** [native Godot episode](../../godot-episode/README.md)
  exercises an actual controller/collision boundary with its own game identity,
  versions and fixtures. Quay Crossing below is a separate paper model of the
  signal → legal command → route → combined threat relationship. A paper result
  does not stand in for the native run.
- **Analysis lab:** [observer-specific views](../../analysis-lab/README.md#observer-specific-views)
  consumes migrated state to produce actor-specific choices and selected
  content; [state migration](../../analysis-lab/README.md#state-migration) covers
  the state boundary. The third-bell gamebook supplies the authored world,
  evidence and scenes. The analysis lab is not a full narrative interpreter for
  this gamebook, and the two artifacts do not share an implicit save schema.

## Method owners

Use [actions and time](../../../skills/gameplay-design/references/actions-and-time.md),
[entities](../../../skills/gameplay-design/references/entities.md), and
[space and situations](../../../skills/gameplay-design/references/space-and-situations.md)
for the action and expressive-play studies. [Inference](../../../skills/game-information-design/references/inference.md)
and [learning and help](../../../skills/game-information-design/references/learning-and-help.md)
own the reasoning and teaching relationships. [World relations](../../../skills/game-world-narrative-design/references/world-relations.md)
and [conditional story](../../../skills/game-world-narrative-design/references/conditional-story.md)
own the encounterable world and surviving dramatic consequences. Those resources
include their public primary-source basis and transfer limits.

All people, rules, quoted lines, observations within the fiction and numerical
cases here are invented. A written fictional testimony is game content; it is
not empirical testimony from a player or a claim about a real culture.
