# Game design

Turn the requested intention into something a participant can do, encounter or
express. Develop the actual object: a rule, place, encounter, scene, sequence,
resource relation, mode of participation or adaptation. A GDD is one possible
delivery format, not a prerequisite.

## Establish the current game

For a new concept, identify the valued relation and constraints that distinguish
a promising direction. For an existing game, read the affected current material
before proposing its replacement. Use [relations and context](references/relations-and-context.md)
when the purpose, participant situation or cross-system dependencies need work.
An established local purpose does not need to be reopened.

Resolve applicable general methods through the explicit
[consumer and Assay contract](references/consumer-and-assay-contract.md).
Assay owns their procedures; this package owns the game distinctions below.
Read that contract on first composition, a changed source/provider, or a handoff
whose game identity or authority is unclear. No automatic install or permission
follows from a peer name.

## Develop a usable direction

1. Express the intention as a relation between available information, an action,
   a consequence and the condition under which it matters. For expressive play,
   this can be a controllable transformation with no victory condition.
2. Where the relation is unresolved, vary a consequential dimension: who knows,
   who acts, what can change, who pays, what persists or when a response is possible.
   Compare genuinely different possibilities; do not require a quota of ideas.
3. Develop a chosen possibility into the smallest whole that exposes its promise:
   provide a starting situation, usable material, an action and its consequences.
   Preserve an interesting finding from an incomplete prototype before polishing.
4. Follow changed relations into dependent material. A new price may change access,
   a new clue may change a character's knowledge, and a new body size may erase an
   encounter's intended escape. Use one current version and set of identities.
5. Return the usable artifact, the basis for the choice, what the actual observations
   establish and the consequential unresolved question. Retaining a working rule,
   ending a campaign or keeping discomfort can be the justified outcome.

Do not turn a convenient proxy into the intended quality: more shared points do
not by themselves create interdependence; more rooms do not establish variety;
finishing a tutorial does not establish learning. The relevant subject method
supplies the constructive distinction, not a universal score.

## Keep the decision revisable

Before accepting a change, inspect one plausible way it could preserve a metric
while destroying the valued relation, and one legitimate alternative that a rigid
rule would reject. Choose the contrast for this object rather than completing all
branches. Use Assay for general evidence, verification and stopping decisions.
For continuation, preserve the current artifact and changed dependencies in the
consumer's existing work record. That record is separate from a game's save.

The method is an evidence-informed design aid. Its behavioral benefit has not
been established by a model campaign. Sources and transfer limits are attached
to the relevant references; evaluation keys are not runtime resources.
